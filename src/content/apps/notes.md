features:
  - title: Shelves and notebooks
    text: Keep notebooks on shelves, each with its own color, texture, title and icon. Pages can be plain, lined, dotted or grid.
  - title: Text with room to work
    text: Format with any font, including ones you import. Highlight in the color you pick, and add tasks you can check off.
  - title: Quick notes
    text: Jot something down without opening a notebook. Quick notes are grouped by date and shown as a list or a grid.
  - title: Made for folds and tablets
    text: On an unfolded phone or a tablet, a notebook opens like a real one, with one page on each side of the seam.
  - title: Sync on your own server
    text: Phone and tablet stay in step within seconds through a PocketBase server you run. Your notes never touch anyone else's cloud.
  - title: Export to a folder
    text: Export by hand whenever you like, or let Notes export automatically to a folder you choose.
  - title: Freeform pages
    text: Fixed-size pages for shapes, arrows, connected nodes, images, voice notes and brush work, in sizes from cover screen to A3.
  - title: Templates
    text: Save any page or quick note as a template, keeping its headers and text.
  - title: Home screen widgets
    text: A shelf of notebooks, a single notebook, your quick notes, or a toolbar for starting something new.
privacy:
  - No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  - Notes are stored on your device. Sync only talks to a server you set up.
  - There's no Quartz account. The only sign-in is to your own sync server, if you use one.

Quartz Notes is for both kinds of writing: the thought you need to get down in five seconds, and the notebook you keep adding to for months. Quick notes stay quick. Notebooks get shelves, page styles and room to spread out on a bigger screen.
